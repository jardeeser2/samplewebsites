FAQ

Answers about who we are, what we sell, and where we deliver.

Where are you located?

Sunrise Home Center is in the Lewiston–Clarkston Valley at 1463 Bridge St., Clarkston, WA 99403 — on the west side of Clarkston across from Walla Walla Community College.

How long have you been in business?

We’ve been in the manufactured home business since 1992, serving Washington and Idaho.

What kind of homes do you offer?

All homes are HUD approved. Ask us about Engineered Certified Energy Star homes, plus Marlette and Champion floor plans in single, double, and triple section layouts.

Do you help with land and financing?

Yes — we offer contractor coordination, land/home coordination, and financing coordination so you’re not figuring it out alone.

What’s included with delivery and setup?

Our prices include delivery and installation within approximately a 100-mile radius service area. That covers Idaho cities like Grangeville, Kamiah, Potlatch, and Plummer, as well as Washington cities like Colfax, Spangle, and Dayton. We’ve delivered nearly to the Canadian border — longer distances may incur extra fees.

What’s the best way to reach you?

Call (509) 758-4000 or 800-701-4001, or email sunrisehomecenter@gmail.com. We’re open Monday–Friday 9–5 and Saturdays 10–4.
