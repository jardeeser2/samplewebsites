Our Approach

How we work with you to design homes and developments that get built.

Robust Systems

We strive for constant improvement and have spent years refining our templates and internal processes. All drawings go through a rigorous internal review process prior to being submitted to council. This ensures that you can expect consistency and accuracy from our drawings, regardless of who has worked on them.

Working with you

We understand that the design process is best as a healthy collaboration. We work together with you to create the best outcome, whether it be for a home or development.

Nailing the brief

We are experts at interpreting a brief, and enjoy taking the time to gain a deep understanding of what you want to achieve from the design process. We understand that the goal is not just to design the dream house, but to build it so you can enjoy it for years to come. We ask the tough questions up front and challenge your ideas, which has resulted in a near-perfect record of houses we design going to construction.

Purpose-built studio

Our design studio has been designed and built to show off some of our favourite building materials to help you select the best products for your home or project.

Creative experts working with you to design the perfect home or development in Wellington or Queenstown.
