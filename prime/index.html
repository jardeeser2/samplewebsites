What we can do for you

Designing homes and developments that work

Designing Your Family Home

We believe everyone should have access to great design. We strive to find ways to create unique and well thought out designs, which are functional and practical to build. The design and consenting process can be intimidating, and we are here to guide you through it so you can focus on the fun parts.

“Our ideas were all over the place! Our designer asked us all the right questions to clarify what we were looking for, then translated that into a design that was perfect for us.” – Geoff & Jen

Maximising the Potential of Your Development

We are experienced in preparing house designs for subdivisions and multi-unit development from 2 lots to over 200. A solid understanding of the council process and strong relationships with local and council planners are key to ensuring that the consenting process runs smoothly.

“Prime Designs showed us options for our site that we didn’t even know we had. We ended up with 2 more lots than expected and the consent process went smoothly.” – Mitchell

Realising Your Vision

We know that it makes it easier to understand a design when you have some great 3D images to support it. We are able to produce high quality visualisation drawings which can give you peace of mind as to what you can expect from the finished product.

“Having the quality of renders that we get from Prime Designs makes it easy to stand out when we are trying to sell our houses off the plans. It also helps our design and build clients to visualise what their finished product will be.” – Rhys, Kiwi Homes / Developments
