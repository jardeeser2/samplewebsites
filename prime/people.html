Our People

We share a common vision – to create great quality designs and deliver a smooth and consistent consenting process.

Mixed skill sets

Our people have a mixture of backgrounds that combine to give us a well rounded understanding of the drawing and construction process. Everyone has their place in the team and we work together to deliver an exceptional result for you.

Bottom-up leadership

We believe that supporting our people is the key to our success. Our leadership structure promotes all members of our team to progress and have unique responsibilities based around their skill sets. Our management team is here to inspire and support our designers and technicians so that they can excel at their work.

Fostering creativity

We promote a fun, approachable culture that values high quality output, while recognising that people do their best work when they can let their hair down and relax when needed.

Pushing for innovation

Our industry is always moving forward and our team keeps their eye firmly on creative new technologies and methods to get the best results for our customers.
