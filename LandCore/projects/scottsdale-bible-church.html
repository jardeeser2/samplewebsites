Scottsdale Bible Church

Scottsdale, AZ

Scottsdale Bible Church (“SBC”) is a 20 acre church campus located at the southeast corner of Shea Boulevard and Miller Road at 7601 E. Shea Boulevard. This major church campus renovation project included modernization of existing buildings, greater functional efficiency, visually stunning spaces, improved pedestrian safety and flow, and further development of site work and landscaping. Changes that are more significant include the demolition of two buildings and a pedestrian bridge, the construction of three new buildings, north parking lot and hardscape improvements.

Landcor Consulting provided a full range of civil engineering services including ALTA/ASCM Land Title Survey, Final Plat, preliminary & final grading & drainage plans, water/sewer plans, demolition plans, and Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plans (SWPPP) in addition to a range of post design civil construction services.
