Barnone

Gilbert, AZ

Barnone is a Craftsmen Community located in Agritopia in Gilbert, AZ. The project involved the adaptive re-use of an existing 1950s Quonset hut and the construction of two new buildings along with complete site/landscape improvements. Barnone provides retail space for local small business owners, restaurateurs, as well as a winery.
