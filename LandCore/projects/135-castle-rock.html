135 Castle Rock

Sedona, AZ

135 Castle Rock is a luxury vacation rental development in Sedona, Arizona, with architectural design by The Ranch Mine. LANDCOR served as the civil engineer of record and provided complete civil design services, including grading and drainage design, utility design/coordination, and agency permitting support. LANDCOR also supported the project during construction with construction administration services.
