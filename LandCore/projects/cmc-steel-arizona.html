CMC Steel Arizona

Mesa, AZ

Landcor has been involved with several on-going projects with CMC Steel Arizona. The existing 75-acre site is a private steel “micro mill” facility for recycling scrap steel. The most current project involved expansion of operations including buildings and infrastructure for administration offices and a fabrication shop. Landcor was responsible for preparation of civil construction documents for site work including new parking lots and driveways; retention and storm drain facilities; and relocation and extension of site utilities.
