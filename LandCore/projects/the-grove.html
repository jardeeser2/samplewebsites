The Grove

Chandler, AZ

This Project is the second phase of a multi-phase church campus development on 20.7 acres in Chandler, Arizona. Site improvements included with the new worship center and classroom buildings consist of a parking lot expansion, access drive to Queen Creek Road, re-configuration of the sports fields and hardscape/landscape improvements. Landcor Consulting provided a full range of civil engineering services from schematic design through completion of construction.
