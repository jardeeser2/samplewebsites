Responsive. Reliable. Experienced.

Landcor Consulting is a Civil Engineering and Land Surveying firm built for clients who need projects done right the first time — and with clarity at every step. Since 2003, we’ve worked with developers, builders, architects, property owners, and design teams who value practical solutions, clean documentation, and a partner who can confidently navigate regulations without slowing the job down.

Our team brings seasoned judgment to every site and every set of plans. We know how to spot issues early, coordinate effectively with agencies, and design solutions that are both constructible and defensible. Whether a project is straightforward or full of constraints, we approach it with precision, creativity, and the kind of regulatory knowledge that only comes from years in the field.

Just as important, we’re responsive and easy to work with. We return calls and emails quickly, stay proactive with clear updates, and make sure you always know what’s happening, what’s next, and what we need to keep things moving.

And because communication only matters if it leads to results, we don’t overpromise. We set realistic schedules, manage risk early, and deliver when we say we will. If something changes — agency comments, site constraints, or scope shifts — you’ll hear it from us early, along with clear options and honest recommendations.

Our goal is simple: expert engineering, clear communication, and integrity — so you can move efficiently, reduce surprises, and make confident decisions.

We are licensed in Arizona, Idaho, and Washington.
